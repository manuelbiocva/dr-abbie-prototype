The NBA method

Developed in our clinics,taught internationally

Dr Abbie Najjarine (Podiatrist) developed the Najjarine Biomechanical Assessment and has lectured throughout Australia, New Zealand, the United Kingdom, Europe and Asia. It assesses alignment from the feet up, and it is the starting point for every treatment plan at Dr. Abbie Clinics.

  • In private practice since 1990
  • Author of The Orthotic Revolution
  • Co-founder and inventor of the patented ICB Dual Density Orthotic
  • Former board member, Australian Podiatry Association (NSW & ACT)
