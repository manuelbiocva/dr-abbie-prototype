MeetArega Sarkisian

Arega Sarkisian (B Pod) is a general treatment podiatrist at The Bowral Foot Clinic, where she provides general foot care.

Arega does not practise at our other clinics.

Arega does not carry out the Najjarine Biomechanical Assessment. For a biomechanical assessment, choose another practitioner.
